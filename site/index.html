Stop switching between Google Analytics properties

Flotilla signs in with your Google account, finds every Google Analytics 4 property you can already read, and puts them all on one screen - live viewers on a world map, with the numbers underneath.

What it does

  • One live map. Viewers active in the last 30 minutes, plotted by city and sized by how many are there. Each property gets its own colour and marker shape, so you can tell them apart at a glance.
  • Trends underneath. Users, new users, sessions, page views, engagement rate and average session length for today, 7, 28 or 90 days - each compared against the previous period.
  • Per property detail. A card per property with its own trend line, plus top pages and traffic channels across everything you own.
  • No per-property setup. Sign in once. Properties you gain access to later show up on their own.

What it accesses, and why

Flotilla requests one Google Analytics permission, and it is read-only:

.../auth/analytics.readonly

View your Google Analytics data. This is the only way to read the reports the dashboard is made of. Without it there is nothing to show.

It is read-only: Flotilla cannot change, create or delete anything in your Google Analytics account.

It also uses your basic Google profile (openid, email, profile) purely to identify your account when you sign in.

How your data is handled

  • Your Analytics figures are shown to you and are never sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Reports are held only in memory to serve your dashboard - they are not written to disk.
  • Your Google refresh token is encrypted at rest so a leaked database does not expose your account.
  • You can disconnect Google or delete your account entirely at any time, from inside the app.
